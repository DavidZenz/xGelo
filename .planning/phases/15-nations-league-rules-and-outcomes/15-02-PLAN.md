## Artifacts this phase produces

- `uefa_nl_make_standings_adapter(match_rows, discipline_points, access_list, group_id, rules)`: closure accepted by `phase14_compute_standings()`.
- `uefa_nl_rank_group(standings, match_rows, discipline_points, access_list, rules)`: Article 15 ordered ranks plus trace/status fields.
- `uefa_nl_group_tiebreak_trace(...)`: deterministic criterion/subset audit rows.
- `uefa_nl_blocked_ordering_result(...)` and `uefa_nl_build_group_standings_state(...)`: typed `ordering_status = blocked`/`missing_rule_input` output and a state wrapper that never passes fake contiguous ranks to `phase14_compute_standings()` and clears provisional ranks before state/outcomes publication.
- `uefa_nl_rank_individual_league(group_standings, match_rows, rules)`: Article 19 per-league ranking with counted/excluded match lineage.
- `uefa_nl_rank_interim_overall(individual_rankings, rules)`: cardinality-aware A 1-16, B 17-32, C 33-48, and D 49 through `48 + nD` rank bands.
- `uefa_nl_select_transition_slots(interim_rankings, euro_playoff_eligibility, rules)`: direct promotion/relegation and A/B, B/C, C/D selector rows.
- Ranking fields: `ranking_scope`, `league`, `group_id`, `team_id`, `group_position`, `computed_rank`, `interim_rank`, `counted_match_ids`, `excluded_match_ids`, `discipline_points`, `access_list_position`, `ordering_status`, `missing_rule_input`, `suppression_reason`, `ruleset_version`, `ruleset_sha256`, and `row_sha256`.
- Transition fields: `stage_id`, `transition_type`, `higher_league_team_id`, `lower_league_team_id`, `team_id`, `higher_league_rank`, `lower_league_rank`, `first_leg_home_team_id`, `eligibility_status`, `selection_status`, `unresolved_reason`, `ruleset_version`, `ruleset_sha256`, and `row_sha256`.
