## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| UEFA JSON/source capture to adapter | Untrusted external fields cross into normalized official fixture/stage records. |
| Accepted tables to ruleset topology | Local data must not change the edition identity or ruleset semantics through row order or unknown IDs. |
| Rules/stage slots to downstream outcomes | Status and provenance determine whether a row is official, projected, unresolved, or suppressed. |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-15-01-S | Spoofing | official stage capture identity | high | mitigate | Require exact edition/stage/source fixture/artifact lineage and reject missing or duplicate official identities. |
| T-15-01-T | Tampering | ruleset and topology serialization | high | mitigate | Canonicalize sorted rule/topology data, hash with SHA-256, and assert reverse-input equality plus known counts. |
| T-15-01-I | Information Disclosure | source/raw paths | medium | mitigate | Carry compact raw hashes and validated relative lineage only; do not publish raw response bodies. |
| T-15-01-R | Repudiation | official versus projected status | high | mitigate | Enforce status-specific required fields and explicit unresolved/suppressed reasons in the stage-slot validator. |
| T-15-01-E | Elevation of Privilege | resource admission | high | mitigate | Keep the Phase 13 five-resource allowlist unchanged and require a separately named stage-capture boundary for any new source fields. |
