## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| Phase 14 forecast artifacts to RNG | Forecast rows and score grids are immutable inputs; simulated outcomes must not rewrite them. |
| Seeded RNG to simulated state | A seed controls all stochastic choices, including draws and penalties, for replayability. |
| Simulated state to projected outputs | Projected rows must not be mistaken for official source facts or feed back into model features. |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-15-03-S | Spoofing | forecast probability view | high | mitigate | Require available status, calibrated primary view, simplex validation, and score-grid identity before sampling. |
| T-15-03-T | Tampering | simulated state and stage draws | high | mitigate | Seed once, derive stable stage/iteration draws, validate legal pairings, and hash input/output projections. |

| T-15-03-D | Denial of Service | malformed score grid or stage graph | medium | mitigate | Validate finite bounded score support, exact fixture identity, legal draw cardinality, and fail before iteration on malformed inputs. |
