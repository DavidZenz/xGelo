|----------|-------------|
| registered inputs -> CLI candidate | Source/config paths, Phase 14 state, Phase 15 handoff, rules, simulation, and flags are untrusted until registered validation passes. |
| candidate outcomes -> accepted output root | Candidate files must be exact-inventory, schema, status, lineage, and hash valid before atomic replacement. |
| CLI/process replay -> filesystem evidence | Dry-run/replay are read-only; fresh child processes must agree byte-for-byte with in-process candidates. |
| full-suite process -> baseline comparator | Exit status, normalized failure identities, and complete output are evidence that must be compared against the recorded baseline without suppressing new failures. |
| outcomes bundle -> later payload consumer | Status, warnings, empty collections, stable IDs, and lineage must remain truthful and renderer-neutral. |
