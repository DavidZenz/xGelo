<threat_model>
## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| Model adapter → common contract | Model-specific output is untrusted until complete-grid, fixture, manifest, and coverage validation passes. |
| Optional restricted snapshot → feature-rich panel | Local restricted inputs may inform declared features but raw data must not enter public/open-core bundles. |
| RNG registry → parallel model branches | Mutable/model-specific random streams can invalidate paired comparisons. |
| Fitting service → registered model family | Convergence fallback or silent predictor changes can impersonate the registered baseline. |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-09-06 | Tampering | score distributions and derived markets | high | mitigate | Full fixed support, tail/sum/finite checks, distribution-derived markets, and `1e-10` reconciliation in `contracts.R`. |
| T-09-07 | Spoofing | adapter fixture/panel identity | high | mitigate | Assert exact registered fixture/team/boundary/panel keys and fail on missing/extra rows before scoring. |
| T-09-08 | Repudiation | model family/formula/convergence | high | mitigate | Frozen registry plus manifests recording formula, predictors, family, theta, convergence, failure, code/package versions, and hashes. |
| T-09-09 | Information Disclosure | feature-rich restricted inputs | medium | mitigate | Panel/license labels, aggregate feature/coverage outputs only, local raw paths excluded from durable public bundles. |
| T-09-10 | Denial of Service | oversized or malformed score grids | medium | mitigate | Fixed `16 x 16` support, expected cell count, finite/range checks, and no adapter-controlled support dimensions. |
| T-09-11 | Tampering | common random-number comparison | medium | mitigate | Seed registry independent of model ID/execution order and deterministic rerun/hash tests. |
</threat_model>
