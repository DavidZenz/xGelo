- **After each non-final wave:** Use the task-level focused commands; do not run the repository-wide suite as an intermediate wave gate.
- **Final post-Wave-12 gate / before `/gsd:verify-work`:** Plan 13-08 completes in Wave 11; Plan 13-06 is Wave 12 and depends on Plans 13-05, 13-08, and 13-12. ## Task-level HUMAN CHECK gate (Plan 13-06-03)

Task 13-06-03 is a blocking HUMAN CHECK. The automated prerequisite `Rscript --vanilla -e 'testthat::test_dir("tests/testthat")'` runs first and remains separate from manual/live evidence; the repository-wide suite is intentionally long-running (approximately 352 seconds in prior Phase 13 execution). The gate is approved only when all three evidence sets below are saved and reviewed:

- **Official live structured replay:** exact `scripts/acquire_uefa_snapshot.R` command with explicit `--fixtures-url`, `--groups-url`, `--standings-url`, and `--results-url` HTTPS values, plus `--status-url` when available, isolated `--output-root`/`--registry-root`, and the resulting normalized accepted `fixtures.csv`, `groups.csv`, `standings.csv`, `results.csv`, `status.csv`, both accepted `source_bundle_manifest.csv` files, `source_artifacts.csv`, `source_bundles.csv`, canonical/derived hash outputs, and post-normalization loader-success output. If status is derived, include its `source_artifact_id` contributor links.
- **Reviewed-fallback replay:** isolated replay command and evidence containing fallback source, retrieval date, reason, operator note, checksum, approval/review state, accepted manifests, source registries, and edition-wide fallback status.
- **Invalid replacement/rollback:** isolated invalid-candidate or injected-failure command and recursive byte/SHA-256 snapshots proving all 14 normalized publication targets, accepted output, source registries, and manifests are unchanged; `competition_editions.csv` has `blocked=TRUE` and `blocked_refresh_batch_id`; `data/competition/registries/refresh_batches/<edition_id>/<refresh_batch_id>/blocked_refresh.json` has matching `refresh_batch_id` and `status=blocked`; `status_history.csv` retains the block event; and explicit recovery plus a later distinct batch are required. Include proof that prior `refresh_batches` records remain outside the accepted replacement target.

Resume only after the three evidence sets and the automated prerequisite are available; deterministic fixture replay is supporting evidence and cannot substitute for this gate.
