| Boundary | Description |
|---|---|
| adapter output → bundle builder | Remote-derived bytes and canonical rows enter durable candidate construction. |
| authority evidence → promotion eligibility | Provider, manual, or fixture evidence attempts to authorize downstream promotion. |
| candidate files → bundle validator | Local files may be tampered, reordered, symlinked, or partially written. |

</threat_model>
