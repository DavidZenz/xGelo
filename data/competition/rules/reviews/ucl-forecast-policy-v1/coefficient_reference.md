# Fixed preseason coefficient evidence

[Official historical coefficient page](https://www.uefa.com/nationalassociations/uefarankings/club/?year=2026)
uses season2025/26, covering2021/22–2025/26. Its nine observed public response
bodies and manual DOM/screenshot are captured with hashes in the evidence
manifest. The current default2026/27 table is a different window and is unused.

[Same-edition AnnexD.2](https://documents.uefa.com/r/Regulations-of-the-UEFA-Champions-League-2026/27/D.2-Reference-periods-for-rankings-Online)
fixes these club coefficients before the2026/27 season. [AnnexD.4](https://documents.uefa.com/r/Regulations-of-the-UEFA-Champions-League-2026/27/D.4-Club-coefficient-calculation-Online)
takes the larger of five-season club points and20% of association points.

[Article18](https://documents.uefa.com/r/Regulations-of-the-UEFA-Champions-League-2026/27/Article-18-Equality-of-points-league-phase-Online)
applies discipline before coefficient. Fixed known coefficients cannot close a
missing disciplinary criterion by themselves. All36 effective values are
distinct, so no AnnexD.8 lower-order coefficient equality criteria are needed
for this roster. A future equal-valued roster blocks pending genuine evidence.

[Official Como profile](https://www.uefa.com/uefachampionsleague/clubs/79946/)
identifies Como79946 as Italian. [UEFA first-entry article](https://es.uefa.com/uefachampionsleague/news/02a9-218315d13f10-e5c278dff851-1000--el-como-en-la-champions-league-2026-27-calendario-partidos-y/)
confirms its first European club competition appearance. The zero prior club
points plus Italy19.989 floor are an explicit D.4 derivation, not an invented
official overall rank; the table leaves Como's official overall rank absent.
