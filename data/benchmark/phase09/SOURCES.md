## Curation rules

- Tournament scope is fixed to the twelve editions in D-01; edition windows are explicit, including `euro2020` played in 2021.
- Group-stage membership is reconstructed as the connected components of the official round-robin fixtures and stored as stable edition-local group IDs. Display letters are deliberately not inferred.
- Stage boundaries follow the registered 64/31/51-match format families. All 630 local result rows reconcile exactly to the local goalscorer event counts.
- Regulation scores count goals through minute 90; final scores preserve the upstream result. Shootout winners are stored independently.
- Every correction below is verified against its named local artifact and SHA-256 before sealing.
